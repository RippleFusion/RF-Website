01

Tech Due Diligence

Better Tech DD helps Private Equity (and corporate) buyers understand the software and systems their acquisition target is running, and identify outsized Tech risks prior to acquisition — enabling better negotiated terms and a clear plan to address risks in the first 100 days post-investment.

It also surfaces Tech-powered Value Creation opportunities pre-acquisition, informing the investment thesis. We bring senior Tech leaders with real operational experience, our own framework, and AI tools to accelerate the work — offering standard assessment packages to meet aggressive deal timelines.

Deliverable

Tech DD Assessment

A point-in-time snapshot identifying pre-acquisition Tech risk.

02

Tech Strategy

Companies 10+ years old with an acquisition or two under their belt often end up with a "spaghetti" of systems, duplicate processes and manual workarounds. If you're planning your next five years of growth and unsure what to invest in, in what order, and at what cost — this is where Dawn Exit helps.

We provide Tech Strategy guidance and execution support: crafting a Tech Roadmap for Value Creation initiatives, prioritising and costing key programmes, running supplier RFPs, interviewing candidates for key roles, and kicking off priority initiatives aligned to the company's goals.

Deliverable

Tech Strategy Assessment

A forward-looking view identifying Tech Value Creation opportunities, including how to supercharge the business with AI.

03

Exit Preparation

The same playbook applies to Exit Preparation. A Growth Company positions itself more favourably with prospective buyers — whether another PE fund or a large corporate — when it has well-organised, up-to-date documentation and a clear roadmap of ongoing Tech initiatives.

Having this ready well ahead of time, and ensuring it's a true reflection of company status, accelerates the buyer's Due Diligence process and compresses the seller's own exit timeline.

Deliverable

Exit Readiness Assessment

A point-in-time snapshot to understand current patterns and maximise future opportunities.
